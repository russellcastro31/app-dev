# My Favorite Movie: Cars – Lightning McQueen
"I am speed!" A famous quote from Lightning McQueen that represents his confidence, determination, and passion for racing.

## About the Movie
**Cars is an animated sports comedy film produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures. It follows Lightning McQueen, a talented race car who learns important lessons about friendship, teamwork, and humility during an unexpected journey to Radiator Springs**.

## Main Characters
1. Lightning McQueen
2. Mater
3. Sally Carrera
4. Doc Hudson
5. Chick Hicks

## Why I Like It
- Exciting racing scenes
- Funny and entertaining characters
- Mater's hilarious personality
- Lightning McQueen's confidence and determination
- Beautiful animation and colorful settings
- Meaningful lessons about friendship and teamwork
- An inspiring story about becoming a better person
